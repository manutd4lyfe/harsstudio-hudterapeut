Welcome to our beauty salon

Beauty & Care is a modern beauty salon where we combine traditional craftsmanship with modern techniques. Our hair salon and skin therapist work together to provide you with a complete beauty experience.

We have over 15 years of experience in the industry and are proud to offer personal service in a relaxed and professional environment. Our team consists of certified hairdressers and skin therapists who continuously train to stay updated with the latest trends and techniques.
